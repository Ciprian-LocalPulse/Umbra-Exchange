# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Umbra Exchange is built in the open, and welcomes contributions from
cryptographers, SOC/OSINT practitioners, and anyone with relevant operational
experience — regardless of background or experience level. We pledge to make
participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, especially on the open design
  questions called out in `docs/PROTOCOL_SPEC.md` and `docs/THREAT_MODEL.md`.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive criticism — per
  `CONTRIBUTING.md`, critique of the protocol and threat model is some of
  the most valuable contribution possible right now.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who will
  eventually rely on it for real threat-intelligence sharing.
- Citing sources for cryptographic claims rather than asserting from
  authority — this is a security-sensitive project, and unreviewed
  cleverness is treated as a liability, not a feature.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion (issues, PRs, or any other project
  space).
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull
requests, discussions) and when an individual is representing the project
in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to
**contact@localpulse.pro**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. Project maintainers are obligated to
respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
